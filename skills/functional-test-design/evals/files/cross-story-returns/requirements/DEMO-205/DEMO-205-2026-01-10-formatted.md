# DEMO-205 — Refund method

Synthetic requirement for skill evaluation. Not from any real system.

- Snapshot date: 2026-01-10

## User Story

As a customer, I want my refund to go back to where I paid so that I do not need to ask for it.

## Entry point

A refund is issued for a returned item.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC 1

A refund is issued to the original payment method.

### AC 2

The refund confirmation email states the refunded amount.
