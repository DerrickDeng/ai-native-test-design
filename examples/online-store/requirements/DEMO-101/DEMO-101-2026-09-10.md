title：Select a delivery method

description：
As a signed-in customer, I want to select a delivery method before placing an order.

Acceptance Criteria (AC)：
* The Delivery section displays Standard and Express.
* Standard delivery takes 5–7 business days and costs $0.
* Express delivery takes 1–2 business days and costs $12.
* Selecting a delivery method updates the order total with the selected delivery cost.
