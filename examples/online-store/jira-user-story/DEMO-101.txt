Scenario: 01 Delivery section displays each available method and its terms
Given customer is signed in to the online store
Given customer is reviewing an order with merchandise total "$40"
When customer opens the Delivery section
Then delivery method "<method>" should be displayed
Then delivery estimate should be "<estimate>"
Then delivery cost should be "<cost>"

Examples:
| method   | estimate          | cost |
| Standard | 5–7 business days | $0   |
| Express  | 1–2 business days | $12  |

Scenario: 02 Selecting a delivery method updates the order total
Given customer is signed in to the online store
Given customer is reviewing an order with merchandise total "$40"
When customer selects delivery method "<method>"
Then the selected delivery cost should be "<cost>"
Then the order total should be "<order_total>"

Examples:
| method   | cost | order_total |
| Standard | $0   | $40         |
